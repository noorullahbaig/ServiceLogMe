# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Service technicians working from phones, and administrators, supervisors, operations staff, and accounts staff working from desktop. Their primary job is to create, complete, retrieve, and report an authoritative Service Note without returning to paper, spreadsheets, or chat messages.

## Product Purpose

ServiceLOGME turns a service visit into one permanent record covering service details, customer contacts, labor, materials, photos, charges, payment, acceptance, and a professional report. Success means a technician can complete the workflow confidently in the field and office staff can trust, find, print, and share the resulting record.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, credible. The product should feel like dependable commercial operations software: restrained, readable, and focused on the current task.

## Anti-references

Avoid prototype or demo cues, decorative SaaS dashboards, invented metrics, glass effects, gradients, card mosaics, marketing copy inside operational workflows, tiny low-contrast labels, ornamental motion, and visible controls without working behavior.

## Design Principles

1. Make the Service Note the center of every workflow.
2. Show clear state, ownership, readiness, and next actions.
3. Preserve trust through accurate totals, immutable completed records, and consistent reports.
4. Design the field workflow for touch and customer handoff, not as a collapsed desktop page.
5. Prefer readable density, continuous sections, and familiar controls over decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast, keyboard navigation, semantics, focus, input labeling, error recovery, reduced motion, and target sizing. Support 200% text scaling and common iPhone and Android viewport sizes without hidden actions or data.
